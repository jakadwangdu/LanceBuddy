Privacy Policy

How LanceBuddy collects, uses, and protects your information

Last updated: August 17, 2026

LanceBuddy ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website lancebuddy.in and use our free lead generation tool.

1. Information We Collect

Personal Information

We collect minimal personal information only when you voluntarily provide it:

  • Email address — When you use the Scout Tool to receive lead results
  • Name and contact details — When you submit the support/contact form
  • Business sector and location — For generating relevant leads (processed locally in your browser)

Automatically Collected Data

  • Usage analytics — Via Google Analytics (GA4) to understand site traffic and improve features
  • Ad interaction data — Via Google AdSense for ad personalization and fraud prevention
  • Browser storage data — Lead notes, preferences, and tool state stored in your browser's localStorage (never sent to our servers)

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Deliver lead generation results to your email
  • Respond to support inquiries
  • Improve website functionality and user experience
  • Display relevant advertisements via Google AdSense
  • Comply with legal obligations

3. Data Storage & Security

Local-first architecture: Your lead data, notes, pipeline status, and preferences are stored exclusively in your browser's localStorage. We do not operate a backend database for user data. This means:

  • Your data never leaves your device unless you explicitly export it
  • We cannot access, view, or recover your saved leads or notes
  • Clearing browser data will permanently delete your local information

Email addresses submitted for lead delivery are processed by our email service provider and not stored on our systems after delivery.

4. Third-Party Services

We integrate the following third-party services:

  • Google AdSense — Displays advertisements; uses cookies for ad personalization. Google's Ad Policy
  • Google Analytics (GA4) — Anonymous usage analytics. Google Privacy Policy
  • EmailJS / FormSubmit — Handles contact form submissions and lead delivery emails
  • Font Awesome, Google Fonts, GSAP, cdnjs — CDN-hosted assets for fonts, icons, and animations

4.1 Advertising Cookies & Personalized Ads

LanceBuddy displays advertisements served by Google AdSense. In accordance with Google's advertising requirements, we disclose the following:

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website and/or other websites on the Internet.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to users based on their visits to LanceBuddy and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • Users may opt out of third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info.
  • Google's use of the DoubleClick DART cookie (or successor technologies) enables it to serve ads to users based on their visit to this site and other sites. Users may opt out of the use of the DART cookie by visiting the Google ad and content network privacy policy at https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

We do not serve non-personalized ads to users in the European Economic Area (EEA), the UK, or Switzerland until consent is obtained, and we honor the consent choices recorded by our cookie consent banner.

5. Cookies & Tracking

We use cookies and similar technologies for:

  • Essential — Theme preference, mobile menu state, consent choice
  • Analytics — Google Analytics (_ga, _gid cookies)
  • Advertising — Google AdSense cookies (e.g., _gads, IDE, NID) for ad personalization, frequency capping, and fraud prevention

You can manage cookie preferences via your browser settings or via the consent banner shown on first visit. Disabling cookies may affect site functionality and ad personalization. Personalized advertising is only enabled after you accept our cookie consent banner; declining limits ads to non-personalized options where applicable.

6. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights to:

  • Access, correct, or delete personal data we hold
  • Object to or restrict processing
  • Data portability
  • Withdraw consent (where applicable)
  • Lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority

Since most data is stored locally in your browser, you can delete it anytime by clearing site data in browser settings.

7. Children's Privacy

LanceBuddy is not directed to children under 13 (or 16 in the EEA). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us data, please contact us.

8. International Transfers

Our third-party providers (Google, EmailJS) may process data in the United States and other countries. They adhere to Standard Contractual Clauses and adequate protection frameworks.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. The "Last updated" date at the top reflects the latest revision. Continued use of the site after changes constitutes acceptance.
