Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about using LanceBuddy for your freelance business.

LanceBuddy gives freelancers free access to verified local business leads with real source links on Google Maps, JustDial, IndiaMART, and more. Unlike expensive tools, it includes lead tracking, email templates, and CSV export at zero cost — perfect for freelancers who need clients without spending on ads.

Enter your target business sector and location. LanceBuddy generates structured leads with phone numbers, source platform links, and business details. You can track each lead's status (New → Contacted → Interested → Converted), add personal notes, generate cold emails, and export everything as a CSV file.

Use the built-in Email Template Generator. Select a lead, enter your name and service offering, and it creates a personalized cold outreach email pre-filled with the lead's details. Copy it and send via your email client. You can also message leads directly on WhatsApp using the one-click WhatsApp button on each card.

Yes, LanceBuddy is 100% free. There are no subscriptions, no hidden limits, and no premium tiers. All features — lead generation, pipeline tracking, email templates, WhatsApp links, notes, and CSV export — are available to everyone at zero cost.

Leads are sourced from public business directories including Google Maps, JustDial, IndiaMART, Sulekha, LinkedIn, and Facebook. Each lead includes a direct clickable link to its original listing so you can verify the business yourself before reaching out.

Click the Notes button on any lead card to reveal the notes area. Type your notes and press Enter to save — they are stored in your browser's local storage and persist even after closing the browser. Access all your saved notes anytime from the Notes section in the navigation.

You can scout any business sector — Cafes, Hotels, Travel Agencies, Software Companies, or type any custom sector like Dentists, Roofers, Gyms, or Photographers. The tool works for any local business type you want to target.

Click the Export CSV button in the leads section. The CSV includes all lead details: business name, phone number, source platform, source URL, snippet, priority status, lead status, and your personal notes — ready to import into any CRM or spreadsheet.
